🔥 Who We Are

Zim.saucy_schools is a premium Zimbabwe school culture platform dedicated to showcasing school life, uniforms, events, and student creativity.
We highlight the best schools in Zimbabwe and allow students to submit their pictures for features on our platform.

🎯 Our Mission

To build the biggest school culture gallery in Zimbabwe 🇿🇼 and connect students through visuals, rankings, and events.
