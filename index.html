📊 School Rankings

🏆 #1 Arundel School

Arundel School continues to stand among Zimbabwe’s most respected private schools because of its outstanding academic performance strong leadership programs elite sporting culture and consistent student excellence. The school is widely recognized for producing confident disciplined and high achieving students.

🔥 #1 B School — Glen View 2 High School

Glen View 2 High School has built a powerful reputation as one of the most respected and fast rising B schools in Harare. The school is known for student talent discipline strong community presence and energetic school culture. Among many B schools in Harare Glen View 2 continues to stand out through consistency creativity and growing academic recognition making it one of the most talked about schools in its category.

📚 Falcon College

Falcon College is admired for its excellent balance between academics sports leadership and student development. The school maintains a strong reputation across Zimbabwe because of its discipline tradition and high performance culture.

⭐ St George's College

St George’s College remains one of Zimbabwe’s most historic and elite boys schools well known for academic excellence rugby success and leadership development. Its long standing tradition continues to attract top performing students.

🎓 Peterhouse Boys

Peterhouse is respected for its world class campus excellent academic programs and strong sporting achievements. The school is considered one of the country’s top institutions for developing future leaders.
